# Enztronic Studio — 3D Office Visual Concept v1

Visual direction and planning only. No application code or browser-renderable model has been created.

## Intended experience

A compact professional web-development studio viewed through an elevated isometric camera on Chrome for Windows desktop. All seven agents are recognizable and easy to locate. The space combines a working-office overview with inspectable real task activity.

## Spatial plan

- A business-operations area for Clara, Nadia and Leo, with a small client conversation nook near the entrance.
- Adrian near a project wall, visually connected to both business operations and website delivery.
- A delivery cluster for Lisa, Sam and Theo, with a shared visual-review surface.
- A central review table for the seven agents and owner.
- A modest coffee point and lounge, with room to move past them.
- Seven individual workstations. A continuous broad walking loop connects desks, meeting space and entry.

The generated image is illustrative; dimensions, exact furniture counts, labels and avatar placement must be reconciled when a precise 3D floor plan is authored.

## Art direction

Warm daylight, pale floor, light oak work surfaces, navy upholstery and restrained cobalt accents. Moderately simplified adult-proportioned 3D avatars retain the portraits' hair, skin tones and clothing. Low partitions and a cutaway roof keep the studio legible. The direction is professional and calm, without score counters or decorative game systems.

## Proposed browser interaction

- Default to an overview; allow mouse pan and wheel zoom.
- Click an agent to open their role, task, outputs, pending decisions and recent work.
- Click a workstation or meeting area to focus the camera.
- Give movement a visible purpose: handoff, review, meeting or a change of work location.
- Keep ambient roaming separate from actual work status; walking does not itself imply progress.
- Use concise names with details on selection. Prevent speech bubbles and labels from covering one another.
- Support reduced motion and a stable overview.
- Preserve a conventional task list and activity log beside the scene.

These are proposed behaviours for review, not currently functioning controls.

## Future 3D production direction

Model a genuine single-floor 3D environment using modular furniture and reusable character rigs. Export optimized glTF/GLB assets for a browser renderer such as Three.js. Prefer simple materials, baked lighting where appropriate and restrained realtime effects. Treat desktop performance as a later measured acceptance criterion, not a capability proven by these images.

Three.js official model-loading guide: https://threejs.org/manual/pages/loading-3d-models.html
