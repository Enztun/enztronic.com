# ENZTRONIC Website Rebrand Guideline

## 1. Project Objective

Rebrand the Enztronic website without redesigning the approved logo.

The new website should position Enztronic as:

> **AI Automation & Digital Product Studio**

The company should be presented as a technical partner that builds:

- AI automations
- Business-process automations
- SaaS products
- Custom business platforms
- Websites and web applications
- WhatsApp Business integrations
- n8n workflows
- CRM and API integrations
- Internal dashboards
- Brand and product systems

The website should no longer look like a generic digital-marketing agency.

---

## 2. Brand Positioning

### Brand

**ENZTRONIC**

### Website Category

**AI Automation & Digital Product Studio**

### SEO Descriptor

**AI Automation, SaaS Development & Business Systems Agency in Indonesia**

### Primary Brand Message

> **We build the digital systems your business runs on.**

### Supporting Framework

> **Brand. Build. Automate.**

### Existing Logo Tagline

> **DIGITAL STRATEGY & AUTOMATION**

The existing tagline may remain inside the logo.

The website messaging may use the stronger positioning:

> **AI Automation & Digital Product Studio**

The logo tagline and website category do not have to be identical.

---

## 3. Approved Logo Structure

The approved Enztronic icon consists of three forward-leaning geometric bars.

### Top Bar

- Royal blue
- Solid geometric shape
- Forward-leaning
- Same shape in light and dark variants

### Middle Bar

- Deep navy on light backgrounds
- White on dark backgrounds
- Contains the approved internal notch

### Bottom Bar

- Deep navy on light backgrounds
- White on dark backgrounds

### Non-Negotiable Geometry

Do not change:

- Width
- Height
- Aspect ratio
- Slant angle
- Spacing
- Alignment
- Internal notch
- Relative size between bars
- Wordmark proportions
- Tagline proportions

The dark-background variant is only a color inversion.

It is not a redesigned logo.

## 5. Color Palette

### Core Colors

| Role | Color | HEX |
|---|---|---|
| Primary accent | Royal Blue | `#2563FF` |
| Structural color | Deep Navy | `#071A36` |
| Light and inverse color | White | `#FFFFFF` |

### Supporting Website Colors

| Role | HEX |
|---|---|
| Soft blue surface | `#EEF4FF` |
| Light neutral background | `#F7F9FC` |
| Border color | `#D8E0EB` |
| Muted text | `#667085` |
| Alternate dark surface | `#0B132B` |

### Recommended Color Balance

- 50% white or light neutral
- 25% deep navy
- 15% soft blue
- 10% royal blue

Royal blue should function as an accent and action color.

Do not make every section bright blue.

---

## 6. Typography

### Official Typeface

**Montserrat**

Use Montserrat consistently throughout the website and brand materials.

Do not alternate between Montserrat and vague labels such as “Geometric Sans.”

Montserrat is a geometric sans-serif, but the official font name must remain explicit.

### Recommended Weights

- Display headings: Montserrat Bold
- Section headings: Montserrat Bold or SemiBold
- Body text: Montserrat Regular
- Emphasized body text: Montserrat Medium
- Buttons: Montserrat SemiBold
- UI labels: Montserrat Medium
- Technical labels: Montserrat Medium, uppercase, increased tracking

### Suggested Desktop Scale

- Display: 64–80 px
- H1: 56–72 px
- H2: 40–52 px
- H3: 28–36 px
- H4: 20–24 px
- Large body: 18–20 px
- Body: 16–18 px
- Small text: 14 px

### Suggested Mobile Scale

- H1: 38–48 px
- H2: 30–38 px
- H3: 24–30 px
- Body: 16–17 px
- Small text: 13–14 px

Do not use body text below 16 px on mobile.

---

## 7. Logo Clear Space

Maintain clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of one icon bar.

No text, border, icon, image, or card edge should enter this area.

### Recommended Minimum Sizes

- Horizontal desktop logo: 220 px wide
- Horizontal mobile logo: 170 px wide
- Print logo: 45 mm wide
- Icon-only digital minimum: 24 px
- Favicon source: 512 × 512 px
- Social-profile source: 1080 × 1080 px

The tagline may be removed when it becomes unreadable at small sizes.

Do not resize the tagline independently.

---

## 8. Logo Misuse

Do not:

- Stretch the logo
- Compress the logo
- Make the icon taller
- Make the icon narrower
- Change the slant angle
- Modify the middle-bar notch
- Change spacing between bars
- Add outlines
- Add shadows
- Add glow effects
- Add bevels
- Add unapproved gradients
- Rotate the icon
- Recolor individual bars
- Use unrelated brand colors
- Rebuild the wordmark with another font
- Move the tagline
- Change wordmark tracking
- Use navy logo elements on navy backgrounds
- Use white logo elements on white backgrounds

---

## 9. Website Personality

The website should feel like:

- Technical
- Precise
- Structured
- Modern
- Reliable
- Scalable
- Systems-oriented
- Business-focused

The website should resemble:

- A digital-product studio
- A business-systems architect
- An AI-automation implementation partner
- A SaaS-development company
- A technical consultancy

It should not resemble:

- A generic social-media agency
- A template seller
- A freelancer portfolio
- A cyberpunk AI startup
- A crypto brand
- A generic outsourcing company

---

## 10. Visual Language

Use:

- Workflow diagrams
- Connected system nodes
- Data pipelines
- Product interfaces
- Dashboard screenshots
- API connections
- Automation sequences
- Before-and-after workflows
- System-architecture diagrams
- Structured grids
- Technical labels
- Real project screenshots

Avoid:

- Robot heads
- Glowing AI brains
- Code rain
- Generic handshake photography
- Random glowing objects
- Excessive neon
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Fake dashboards
- Futuristic city imagery
- Decorative graphics with no functional meaning

## 11. Layout System

### Grid

- Use a 12-column desktop grid
- Maximum content width: 1280 px
- Standard content width: 1180–1240 px
- Reading width: 720–800 px

### Spacing

Use an 8 px base spacing system:

- 4 px
- 8 px
- 12 px
- 16 px
- 24 px
- 32 px
- 48 px
- 64 px
- 80 px
- 96 px
- 128 px

Avoid arbitrary spacing values.

### Border Radius

- Small: 8 px
- Standard: 12 px
- Large: 20 px
- Pill: 999 px

Do not make every card excessively rounded.

---

## 12. Buttons

### Primary Button

- Background: `#2563FF`
- Text: `#FFFFFF`
- Font: Montserrat SemiBold
- Radius: 10–12 px
- No gradient required

### Secondary Button on Light Background

- Transparent background
- Deep-navy border
- Deep-navy text

### Secondary Button on Dark Background

- Transparent background
- White border
- White text

### Tertiary Action

- Royal-blue text
- Optional arrow
- No filled background

Do not place more than two dominant CTAs in one section.

## 17. Icons and Illustrations

Icons should be:

- Geometric
- Thin or medium line weight
- Consistent
- Simple
- Royal blue or deep navy
- Monochrome within each component

Do not mix:

- Outline icons
- Cartoon icons
- 3D icons
- Photorealistic icons
- Multi-colored icon packs

Illustrations should explain systems and workflows rather than serve as decoration.

---

## 18. Motion

Use:

- Subtle fades
- Short slide transitions
- Workflow-line animation
- Node activation
- Data movement
- Process reveals
- Button feedback

Avoid:

- Constant floating objects
- Heavy parallax
- Large background videos
- Distracting particles
- Long entrance animations
- Motion without meaning

Support `prefers-reduced-motion`.

---

## 19. Accessibility

Requirements:

- Meet WCAG AA contrast
- Use visible keyboard focus states
- Keep mobile body text at 16 px or larger
- Use semantic heading structure
- Add labels to form inputs
- Use descriptive CTA text
- Do not rely on color alone
- Add alt text to informative images
- Support reduced motion
